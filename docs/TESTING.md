# Testing Tisma Scanner (beta)

Thank you for testing it. It takes 15 minutes. *Español: [PROBAR.md](PROBAR.md)*

The app is in Spanish; the labels below are quoted as it shows them.

## What you need

- An Android 11 or later phone, 64-bit (nearly all since 2019).
- A Tisma account with 3D scans.
- An ordinary printer and a ruler.
- A small part you can measure (with calipers, ideally): a box, a printed part, a remote control…

## 1. Install

Open `TismaScanner-….apk` on the phone and allow "install unknown apps". An earlier version is updated in place.

## 2. Print the mat

`tapetes/` has it in every size: use the one for your paper (A4, Letter, A5…). Print the **PDF** at **"Actual
size" or "100 %"** (not "Fit to page"). Measure the bar at its edge with the ruler: it must be **100 mm** (50 mm
on A6). Otherwise the part will come out bigger or smaller by the same proportion.

## 3. Scan

1. Put the part in the middle of the mat, in good even light (no direct sun). If it is plain or shiny, pick one
   with some pattern or texture for this test.
2. Open the app: ⚙ → **Nube** (Cloud) → **Entrar** (Sign in) with your account. Go back with **←**.
3. The app opens in **"Vídeo 360"** (the switch at the bottom; leave it so). Put the part inside the frame and tap
   the **red button**.
4. **Walk slowly around** the part, at 2 or 3 heights, with the whole part on screen. If it says **"Más
   despacio"** (slower), slow down. Under the button you see the **% covered** ("% cubierto"): go on past **75 %**.
5. Tap the **red button** again to finish, then **→**.
6. In "Tu pieza" (your part), tap **"Crear modelo final en Tisma Cloud"** (make the final model). You can lock
   the phone or leave the app: it carries on, and resumes by itself if the network drops. It takes several
   minutes.
7. At the end, **"Pieza STL"** (STL part) appears with the measures.

## 4. Tell us

- The **measures** the app gave and the ones you measured (length × width × height, in mm).
- The **phone** (make and model) and the **paper size** of the mat.
- Anything that failed or confused you: what you did, what you expected, what happened. A screenshot helps.
- If the app closed or did something odd: ⚙ → **Registro** (log) → **Compartir registro de diagnóstico** (share the diagnostic log) and send us the file.

Your location never leaves the phone: Tisma Cloud only gets the photos.
